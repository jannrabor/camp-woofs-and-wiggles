About Us

Camp Woofs and Wiggles is your dog’s home away from home while you are at work or on vacation. Located in Lincoln, Nebraska, Camp Woofs and Wiggles offers a variety of indoor and outdoor spaces for your dog to run, play, and adventure with their new-found friends. Our trained staff will ensure that your furry family member remains under a watchful eye and will have all of their needs met whether they are here for a day or a week. Additional services include training, bathing/grooming, and pup parties— hosted birthday parties for your pup and their friends.

Well-Trained Staff

Well-trained staff that takes care of your dog's needs. Staff helping with administering medication and correcting food restrictions.

Comfortable Private "Bunks"

Personal private "bunks" for your dog can reset and feel safe. Added cameras so you can watch your dog from wherever you are!

Great Facilities

Clean, well supervised facilities for your dog to have fun and socialize in a safe environment.

We feel it’s important that we offer potential clients a day-in-the-life view of what our furry friends experience while they are at our camp, whether it's playing, exploring, cuddling, or napping!
