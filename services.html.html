Pricing

We offer affordable and flexible pricing options for our dog services, so you can choose the best plan for your budget and your pup’s needs. Whether you need dog walking, grooming, training, or boarding, we have a package that suits you and your furry friend. Note: All campers must be vaccinated, be spayed/neutered, and pass the free temperament assessment (free 1/2 day boarding session).

Half Day (1 dog)

$20/day

  • Indoor and outdoor adventure areas
  • Nap time campers
  • Free medication administration
  • Individual attention from our trained camp counselors
  • *Add $10 per dog, per full or half day

Full Day (1 dog)

$30/day

  • Indoor and outdoor adventure areas
  • Nap time campers
  • Midday snack for camper
  • Free medication administration
  • Individual attention from our trained camp counselors
  • *Add $10 per dog, per full or half day

Nightly Overnight Camp Rate (1 dog)

$40/day

  • All the services we provide to our full-day campers
  • Private “bunks” for our overnight campers with individual cameras that allow parents to check in on their pups
  • A free bath and nail trim for guests staying 3 or more days
  • *Add $15 per dog, per day in a shared bunk (Max 3)
